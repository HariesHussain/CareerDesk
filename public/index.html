Automated Intelligence

How CareerDesk Works Under The Hood

A continuous automated pipeline ensuring you discover every high-stakes national opportunity weeks before registration deadlines close.

STEP 01

Multi-Source Scraping

Our automated crawlers aggregate listings from Brabble, Devfolio, Unstop, MLH, GSoC, and collegiate tech fests every 6 hours.

STEP 02

AI Normalization

Dates, eligibility criteria, cash prizes (in ₹ INR), and event modes (Online / In-Person) are parsed and deduplicated with precision.

STEP 03

Direct Official Link

We never collect intermediary registration fees. You are handed off directly to the official organizer registration portal.

STEP 04

Kanban Milestone Tracking

Save events to your private wishlist, manage rounds, and track team deadlines through our drag-and-drop Kanban tracker.
